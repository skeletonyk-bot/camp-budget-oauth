Privacy Policy — Camp Budget

Last updated: April 26, 2026

Camp Budget is a personal-use macOS application for tracking the developer/owner's own bank transactions. It has exactly one intended user — the person who installed it. This policy describes what data the application handles and how.

Who runs this app

Camp Budget is operated by Dr. Ke Yu as a personal project. Contact: skeleton.yk@gmail.com.

What data is collected

  • Bank transaction data — account balances, transaction amounts, merchant names, dates, and categorization metadata — fetched from your bank via Plaid using credentials you authorize through Plaid Link.
  • Plaid access tokens — opaque strings issued by Plaid that let the application fetch transactions on your behalf.
  • User-defined labels — categories, tags, and rules you create within the app to organize your transactions.

Where data is stored

  • All transaction and configuration data is stored locally on your Mac in a SwiftData database at ~/Library/Application Support/CampBudget/.
  • Plaid access tokens are stored in the macOS Keychain, encrypted by your macOS account password and (on Apple Silicon) the Secure Enclave.
  • No transaction data is uploaded to any server other than Plaid (which is the source of the data, not a destination).

Who data is shared with

Camp Budget shares data only with Plaid, and only as needed to fetch your transactions on your behalf. Plaid's handling of that data is governed by Plaid's own privacy policy, available at https://plaid.com/legal/. No advertising networks, analytics providers, or other third parties receive data from Camp Budget. The application itself sends no telemetry.

How long data is kept

Transaction data is retained locally on your Mac indefinitely until you delete the application's data folder or unlink the relevant Plaid institution. There is no remote data to retain.

How to delete your data

  • In the app: Settings → Accounts → Unlink an institution. This removes the Plaid access token from your Keychain and stops further fetches.
  • To remove all locally stored transaction data: delete ~/Library/Application Support/CampBudget/.
  • To revoke Plaid's continued access to a bank from Plaid's side, visit https://my.plaid.com.

Children's privacy

Camp Budget is not intended for use by children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from children.
