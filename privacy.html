Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 18, 2026  ·  Last updated: June 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy (the “Policy”) explains how Dr. Ke Yu, the operator of Camp Budget (“Camp Budget,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), collects, uses, discloses, retains, and protects personal information in connection with the Camp Budget applications for macOS and iOS and any related services (collectively, the “Service”). For the purposes of applicable data protection laws, the operator acts as the controller of the personal information described below. By creating an account or using the Service, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Policy.

Summary

This summary is provided for convenience only and does not replace or modify the full Policy below.

  • We access your financial account and transaction information through Plaid in order to operate the Service’s budgeting and analysis features.
  • Your information is stored and processed in the cloud using Google Firebase (Google Cloud Platform), located in the United States.
  • Certain features rely on Google’s Gemini artificial-intelligence models; when used, the relevant transaction information (and receipt images you provide) is transmitted to Google for processing.
  • We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.
  • You may unlink accounts, request deletion of your information, and exercise other rights as described in Section 11.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal information:

(a) Account and Authentication Information

When you register, we collect the email address and password you use to create and access your account. Authentication is managed through Google Firebase Authentication.

(b) Financial Account Information

When you link a financial institution through Plaid, we collect information about your connected accounts, including the institution name, account name, the masked (partial) account number, account type, and current and available balances.

(c) Transaction Information

For each transaction, we collect the amount, dates (including authorized and posted dates), merchant name, original description, and assigned category, together with additional details made available by your institution or Plaid, such as the city and region, time, payment channel, counterparties, transfer or payment details, and currency. We also collect information about recurring charges and income identified for your accounts.

(d) Information You Provide

We collect the categories, tags, rules, budgets, saved views, and other content you create within the Service, as well as receipt images you choose to upload.

(e) Technical and Diagnostic Information

We and our infrastructure providers process limited technical and diagnostic information, such as error and event logs, as necessary to operate, maintain, and secure the Service. The Service does not use third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking technologies.

2. Sources of Information

We obtain personal information (i) directly from you when you register, configure, or use the Service; (ii) from your financial institutions through Plaid Inc. (“Plaid”), which connects to your accounts at your direction; and (iii) automatically through your use of the Service, as described above.

3. How We Use Information

We use personal information to:

  • provide, operate, and maintain the Service, including importing, updating, categorizing, and synchronizing your transactions across your devices;
  • generate budgets, trends, summaries, forecasts, and other insights you request;
  • authenticate users and secure accounts, and to detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, or technical issues;
  • respond to your inquiries and provide support; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations and enforce our terms.

Where required by applicable law, we rely on the following legal bases for processing: performance of our agreement with you; your consent (which you may withdraw at any time); our legitimate interests in operating and improving the Service; and compliance with legal obligations.

4. Artificial Intelligence Features

Certain features of the Service are powered by Google’s Gemini artificial-intelligence models, accessed through Google Firebase. These features include automatic naming and categorization of transactions, monthly insights and forecasts, detection of unusual charges and recurring subscriptions, the natural-language assistant, budget suggestions, and reading receipt images. When these features operate, the relevant transaction information — and, where applicable, the receipt image you provide — is transmitted to Google for processing in order to generate the result. Google’s processing of such information is governed by Google’s privacy terms, available at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

5. How We Disclose Information

We disclose personal information only as described in this Policy:

  • Service providers. We share information with the providers that enable the Service: Plaid, which connects to your financial institutions and supplies account and transaction data (see Plaid’s privacy policy); and Google (Firebase and Gemini), which provides hosting, authentication, data storage, and the artificial-intelligence processing described in Section 4 (see Google’s privacy policy). These providers are authorized to use the information only to provide services to us.
  • Legal and protective disclosures. We may disclose information if we believe in good faith that doing so is necessary to comply with applicable law, legal process, or governmental request; to enforce our agreements; or to protect the rights, property, or safety of any person.
  • Business transfers. If the Service is involved in a merger, acquisition, or transfer of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this Policy.

We do not disclose your information to advertising networks, data brokers, or third-party analytics providers.

6. We Do Not Sell Your Information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not “share” it for cross-context behavioral advertising, as those terms are defined under applicable privacy laws. We do not use your financial information to build advertising profiles or for any advertising purpose.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide the Service. When you unlink a financial institution, the associated account and transaction information is deleted from our active systems. When you request deletion of your account, we will delete the personal information associated with it, including stored access credentials. Residual copies may persist in routine backups for a limited period before being overwritten, and we may retain information where required to comply with legal obligations or resolve disputes.

8. Data Security

We implement administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect your information, including:

  • encryption of information in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest within our cloud infrastructure;
  • access controls that restrict each user’s data to that user’s authenticated account;
  • storage of the credentials used to access your financial institutions on secured servers, never on your device or within the App.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials.

9. International Data Transfers

The Service is operated from, and information is stored and processed in, the United States. If you access the Service from outside the United States, you understand that your information will be transferred to, stored, and processed in the United States, where data-protection laws may differ from those in your jurisdiction.

10. Children’s Privacy

The Service is not directed to, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from, children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). If we learn that we have collected such information, we will delete it. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us using the details below.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, update, port, or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. You can access and modify much of your information directly in the App, unlink any institution at any time, and request full deletion as described in Section 12. To exercise any right, contact us at the address below; we will respond as required by applicable law. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

12. Managing and Deleting Your Information

  • Unlink an institution. In the App’s Settings, unlink a linked institution to remove its associated accounts and transactions from our systems and stop further updates.
  • Revoke institutional access. To terminate Plaid’s ongoing connection to a financial institution, visit Plaid’s portal at https://my.plaid.com. Unlinking within the App removes data from the Service but does not, by itself, revoke the connection maintained at your institution.
  • Delete your account. To request deletion of all remaining personal information associated with your account, including stored access credentials, email us at skeleton.yk@gmail.com.

13. Third-Party Services

The Service relies on and links to third-party services, including Plaid and Google. Your use of those services and any information you provide to them are governed by their respective terms and privacy policies. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third parties.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will post the revised Policy at this location and update the “Last updated” date above. Your continued use of the Service after a revision takes effect constitutes your acknowledgement of the updated Policy.

15. Contact Us

If you have questions, requests, or complaints regarding this Policy or your personal information, contact the operator, Dr. Ke Yu, at skeleton.yk@gmail.com.
