Privacy Policy

Your data, in plain terms

Last updated June 18, 2026

Camp Budget is a personal budgeting app for Mac and iPhone. It connects to your bank and credit-card accounts to import your balances and transactions, then helps you organize, budget, and understand your spending. This page explains what information the app handles and the choices you have.

The short version

  • We read your account balances and transactions (through Plaid) so the app can organize and analyze your spending.
  • Your information is stored securely in the cloud, on Google’s Firebase platform, so it stays in sync across your devices.
  • Some features use Google’s AI to categorize, summarize, and answer questions — to do that, your transaction details (and a receipt photo, if you add one) are sent to Google.
  • We never sell your information or use it for advertising.
  • You can unlink an account or delete your data at any time.

1 Who runs Camp Budget

Camp Budget is an independent app operated by Dr. Ke Yu. If you have any questions about your privacy, email skeleton.yk@gmail.com.

2 What information we collect

  • Your accounts — the bank or card name, the account name, the last few digits of the account number, the account type, and your current and available balances.
  • Your transactions — the amount, date, merchant, and the original description, along with the category and the extra details your bank provides, such as the city and time of a purchase.
  • Recurring charges — subscriptions and regular payments the app identifies for you.
  • Things you create — the categories, tags, rules, budgets, and saved searches you set up in the app.
  • Your sign-in — you create an account with an email address and password so your data is private to you.
  • Receipt photos — only if you choose to add a photo of a receipt to a transaction.

3 How we use your information

We use your information only to run the app for you: importing and updating your transactions, sorting them into categories, tracking your budgets, and showing trends, summaries, and answers. We don’t use it for any unrelated purpose.

4 AI features

Several features are powered by Google’s Gemini AI: automatic naming and categories, monthly insights and forecasts, finding unusual charges and subscriptions, the “Ask” assistant, budget suggestions, and reading receipt photos. When these features run, the relevant transaction details — and the receipt image, if you scan one — are sent to Google to produce the result. Google’s handling of that data is described in Google’s Privacy Policy.

5 Who we share information with

We share your information only with the services that make the app work, and only as needed:

  • Plaid connects to your bank and provides your balances and transactions. See Plaid’s privacy policy.
  • Google (Firebase & Gemini) securely stores your data and powers the AI features above. See Google’s privacy policy.

We do not share your information with advertisers, data brokers, or analytics trackers, and the app contains no third-party tracking tools. Your financial data is never sold or used for advertising.

6 How your data is kept safe

  • All information travels between the app and our servers over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.
  • Your data is tied to your account, and only you can access it.
  • The secure keys that let the app fetch data from your bank are kept on our server and are never stored on your device or shown in the app.

7 How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as you use the app. If you unlink an account or ask us to delete your data, it’s removed as described below. If you stop using the app without unlinking, your data stays stored until you delete it or ask us to.

8 How to delete your data

  • Unlink an account — in the app’s Settings, unlink an institution. This removes that account’s transactions and balances and stops further updates.
  • Cut off bank access — to fully end a connection at your bank, visit Plaid’s portal at my.plaid.com. (Unlinking in the app removes the data but doesn’t end the bank connection on its own.)
  • Delete everything — email skeleton.yk@gmail.com and we’ll delete all remaining data tied to your account, including the stored access keys.

9 Children’s privacy

Camp Budget is not intended for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from children.

10 Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we’ll post the new version on this page and update the “Last updated” date above.

11 Contact

Questions or requests? Email skeleton.yk@gmail.com and we’ll be glad to help.
