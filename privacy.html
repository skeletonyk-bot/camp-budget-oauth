Privacy Policy — Camp Budget

Last updated: June 18, 2026

Camp Budget is a personal spending and budgeting app for macOS and iOS, operated by Dr. Ke Yu. It connects to your bank and credit-card accounts through Plaid to import your balances and transactions, stores that data in a secure cloud backend so it stays in sync across your devices, and uses AI to categorize, name, and analyze your spending. This policy describes what data the service handles, where it is stored, who it is shared with, and how you can delete it.

Who runs this app

Camp Budget is operated by Dr. Ke Yu as a personal project. Contact: skeleton.yk@gmail.com.

What data is collected

  • Account data — for each linked account: the institution name, account name, the masked account number (last digits only), account type (checking, savings, credit, other), and current and available balances.
  • Transaction data — for each transaction: amount, date, authorized/posted date, merchant name, the original bank description, and Plaid's spending category. Camp Budget also stores the additional detail Plaid provides about a transaction, including the city and state/region where it occurred, the payment channel (in store, online, etc.), counterparties (the underlying merchant, marketplace, or app), payment and transfer details (such as check number or payee/payer on transfers), the time of day, and the currency if it is not US dollars.
  • Recurring streams — recurring charges and income that Plaid identifies (merchant, typical amount, and frequency), used to surface your subscriptions.
  • Plaid access tokens — opaque credentials issued by Plaid that allow the backend to fetch your balances and transactions on your behalf.
  • Sign-in identity — you create an account with an email address and password, managed by Firebase Authentication, which identifies and secures your data.
  • Data you create in the app — categories, tags, rules, budgets, saved views/searches, and category overrides you set up to organize your transactions.
  • Receipt images (only if you use this feature) — if you attach a photo of a receipt to a transaction, that image is processed to read its details (see "AI features" below).

Where data is stored and processed

Camp Budget uses a cloud backend built on Google Cloud Platform / Firebase, hosted in the United States. Your data is not kept only on your device — it is stored in the cloud so it can sync across your Mac and iPhone:

  • Your balances, transactions, and the data you create are stored in a Google Cloud Firestore database, under records keyed to your own account (users/<your-account-id>/…).
  • Linking, token exchange, and transaction syncing are performed by Firebase Cloud Functions that communicate with Plaid on your behalf. The Plaid API credentials used by those functions are held in Google Secret Manager.
  • Your Plaid access tokens are stored in a server-only area of the database that the Camp Budget apps cannot read; only the backend functions can use them. Database security rules restrict every signed-in user to their own data and deny client access to the stored tokens.
  • All communication between the apps and the backend uses encrypted HTTPS connections.

AI features and how your data is used by them

Camp Budget uses Google's Gemini models, accessed through Firebase AI, to power its smart features. To generate a result, the relevant data is sent to Google's Gemini API. Specifically:

  • Automatic naming and categorization — merchant names, descriptions, and Plaid categories are sent to Gemini to produce clean transaction names and suggested categories. Naming runs automatically when your transactions load or refresh.
  • Insights, forecasts, anomaly and subscription detection — your transactions and category spending totals are sent to Gemini to generate monthly insights, end-of-month forecasts, unusual-activity flags, and a list of recurring subscriptions.
  • "Ask" assistant and budget suggestions — when you ask a natural-language question or request budget suggestions, your question and the matching transactions or spending totals are sent to Gemini to write the answer.
  • Receipt scanning — if you attach a receipt image, the image is sent to Gemini to extract the merchant, amount, and date.

Google's processing of data sent to Gemini is governed by Google's terms, available at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Who data is shared with

Camp Budget relies on the following service providers (sub-processors) to operate, and shares data with them only as needed to provide the service:

  • Plaid — connects to your financial institution and supplies your balances and transactions. Plaid's handling of that data is governed by Plaid's own privacy policy at https://plaid.com/legal/.
  • Google Cloud Platform / Firebase — provides the authentication, database (Firestore), serverless functions, and secret storage that host your data, and the Gemini AI models that power the features described above.

No advertising networks, data brokers, or analytics third parties receive your data, and the app includes no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. Your financial data is never sold or shared for advertising.

How long data is kept

Your account and transaction data is retained in the cloud database until you unlink the institution or request deletion (see below). If you stop using the app without unlinking, your data remains stored until you delete it or ask us to.

How to delete your data

  • Unlink an institution — in the app, go to Settings and unlink a linked institution. This deletes that institution's accounts and transactions from the cloud database and stops further syncing.
  • Revoke Plaid's access to your bank — to cut off Plaid's ongoing access to a financial institution, visit Plaid's user portal at https://my.plaid.com. Note that unlinking inside Camp Budget removes the data from the app but does not by itself revoke Plaid's connection at the bank.
  • Delete everything — to have all remaining data associated with your account deleted, including any stored Plaid access tokens, email skeleton.yk@gmail.com and it will be removed from the backend.

Security

  • Access tokens are stored server-side and are never exposed to the apps.
  • Database rules isolate each user to their own data.
  • Plaid API credentials are held in Google Secret Manager.
  • All traffic between the apps and the backend is encrypted over HTTPS.

Children's privacy

Camp Budget is not intended for use by children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from children.
