How it works

Connect securely

Link your accounts through Plaid, the trusted service banks use for secure connections. Camp Budget never sees your bank password.

We do the tidying

Your transactions are imported, named, and categorized automatically — and kept up to date across your devices.

You stay in control

Set budgets, explore trends, snap a receipt, or just ask a question. Unlink any account whenever you like.

Your data is yours

Camp Budget uses your financial data only to run the app. It’s never sold or used for advertising, it travels over secure connections, and you can delete it at any time.
