Camp Budget

A personal spending & budgeting app for macOS and iOS.

Camp Budget connects securely to your bank and credit-card accounts through Plaid to import your transactions and balances, keeps them in sync across your devices, and uses AI to help you understand and plan your spending.

What it does

  • Automatic import — pulls in transactions and balances from your linked accounts and keeps them up to date.
  • Smart categorization & naming — cleans up cryptic transaction descriptions and sorts spending into categories automatically.
  • Budgets — set per-category budgets, with AI-suggested targets based on your spending.
  • Trends & insights — see spending over time, monthly insights, and end-of-month forecasts.
  • Anomaly & subscription detection — flags unusual charges and surfaces recurring subscriptions, including the irregular ones.
  • Ask — a natural-language assistant: ask questions like "how much did I spend on groceries last month?" and get an answer.
  • Receipt scanning — attach a photo of a receipt and have its details read automatically.
  • Organize your way — tags, rules, and saved views to tailor how transactions are grouped.

Available on macOS and iOS, with your data synced between them.

Your data

Camp Budget accesses your account balances and transactions only to power these features. Your data is stored in a secure cloud backend (Google Firebase) and the app's AI features are powered by Google's Gemini models. Your financial data is never sold or shared for advertising. See our Privacy Policy for the full details of what is collected, where it is stored, and how to delete it.
