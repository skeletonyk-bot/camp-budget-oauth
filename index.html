Camp Budget

A personal spending & budgeting app for macOS and iOS.

Camp Budget connects securely to your bank and credit-card accounts through Plaid to import your transactions and balances, then helps you understand and plan your spending.

What it does

  • Automatically imports and categorizes your transactions
  • Tracks budgets and shows spending trends over time
  • Surfaces insights, recurring charges, and anomalies

Your data

Camp Budget accesses your account balances and transactions only to power these features. Your financial data is never sold or shared for advertising. See our Privacy Policy for details.
